# Credits and Licences

**Emogy: The Memory Game**

## Audio

Every sound effect and every music loop in Emogy is original. They are synthesised on your device from mathematical oscillators and deterministic noise. No recordings, sample packs or third-party audio files are included, so no attribution is required.

## Emoji

Emoji are standard Unicode characters. Their artwork is drawn by your device's system emoji font. Emogy does not bundle any emoji image set. Emoji your device cannot draw are hidden automatically so you never see empty boxes.
