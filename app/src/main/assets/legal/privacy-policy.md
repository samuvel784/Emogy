# Privacy Policy

**Emogy: The Memory Game**
_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

This policy applies to the Emogy Android app (package `com.vurka.emogy`), published by Vurka ("we", "us"). In short: **Emogy collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It works completely offline.**

## Summary

* No account, sign-in or profile.
* Works completely offline.
* No internet permission, so the app cannot transmit data even in principle.
* All game data stays in the app's private storage on your device.
* The only permission is `VIBRATE`, used for haptic feedback.

## Information we collect

**None.** Emogy has no servers, no account system and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app is sent off your device. There is no advertising ID, no analytics and no tracker of any kind. We do not know who you are, how you play or which device you use.

## Information stored on your device

To make the game work, Emogy saves the following in its private app storage:

* **Progress:** completed levels, stars, best times, best move counts and your coins.
* **Rewards:** hint inventory, owned cosmetics (card backs, card themes, boards and effects), claimed task and login rewards, and the progress of daily, weekly and monthly tasks.
* **Login calendar and streak:** the last local calendar date you opened the app, your login streak and which weekly login rewards you claimed. This is a local counter based on your device's date. It is not an account and is never uploaded.
* **Emoji album:** the emoji you have unlocked by matching pairs.
* **Daily Challenge:** the stored board day, best time and moves, completion count and daily streak.
* **Settings:** sound on/off, master, music and effects volume, haptic feedback, light/dark theme mode, selected cosmetics, Zen mode and Assist mode.
* **First-run flag:** whether the welcome sheet has already been shown.
* **Unfinished run:** a snapshot of a level in progress so it can be restored after the app closes. It expires after 24 hours.
* **Statistics:** games played, perfect games, card flips and other counters shown on the Statistics screen.
* **Generated audio cache:** the app creates its own sound effects and music on your device and keeps them in its cache. They contain no personal information and Android may delete them at any time.

This data is used only to run the game and restore your progress. It stays inside the app's private sandbox, which other apps cannot read. We cannot see it and we do not receive a copy.

Because rewards depend on your device's date, the app compares dates to detect a clock that was moved backwards. This check happens only on your device.

## How to delete your data

* **Settings > Reset All Game Progress** clears progress, coins, hints, owned cosmetics, task and login state, the emoji album, daily results and statistics. It keeps your sound, volume, haptics, theme mode, Zen and Assist settings.
* **Android Settings > Apps > Emogy > Storage > Clear storage** removes everything.
* **Uninstalling the app** removes everything stored by the app on that device.

Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Android backup

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, Android may include Emogy's progress and settings in the backup of your device stored in **your own Google Account**, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is performed by Android and Google under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We cannot access, read or delete those copies. You can turn backup off or delete existing backups in your device's Android settings or your Google Account settings.

## Permissions

Emogy requests exactly one Android permission:

* `VIBRATE`: to vibrate briefly on taps, matches and mistakes when haptic feedback is enabled. It is a normal permission and shows no prompt.

The app does **not** request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, notifications or any other permission.

## Third parties and purchases

* **Offline App.** Works completely offline without external servers.
* **No real-money purchases.** Coins are earned by playing, cannot be bought with real money, cannot be transferred or cashed out and have no monetary value. The Coin Store only exchanges those in-game coins for in-game items.
* **No third-party services.** The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, social or cloud SDKs and shares no data with anyone.
* **Links you choose to open.** The About screen has optional buttons to share the app, rate it on Google Play or email support. These hand over to the Android share sheet, the Play Store app or your email app. Anything you send that way is governed by those apps' own policies.

## Children's privacy

Emogy is intended for a **general audience aged 13 and over** and is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and contains no ads, real-money purchases, chat or user-generated content. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us at the address below; however, the app itself has no way to send us data.

## Security

Because Emogy does not transmit data, there is no data in transit to intercept and no server to breach. Locally stored game data is protected by Android's app sandbox and, if you enable it, by your device's lock screen and encryption.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, we cannot access, correct, export or delete it on your behalf. You are in control of the data on your device through the steps above. If you are in a region with data-protection law (for example the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA), these rights are met by design: no personal data is collected, sold or shared.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app update and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
